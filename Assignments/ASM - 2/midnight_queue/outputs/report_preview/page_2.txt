What happened at n=10,000 (fastest / slowest): C1: Merge Sort / Insertion Sort; C2: Naive Quick Sort / Insertion Sort; C3: Insertion Sort / Naive Quick Sort;
C4: Merge Sort / Naive Quick Sort; C5: Insertion Sort / Naive Quick Sort; C6: Hardened Quick Sort / Insertion Sort. Insertion Sort is linear on pre-ranked C3
but quadratic on C4; the reverse and duplicate-heavy cases separate n log n from quadratic behaviour. On C5, 1% displacement already creates 34,333,390
inversions at n=100,000.
Theory versus measurement. At 10k, insertion C1/C4 makes 24,987,904/49,995,000 comparisons, with medians 22.36 s/48.98 s; counts match the inversion
model. Clash: Heap/C1 falls from 12.71 s at 100k to 9.72 s at full size while comparisons rise 3.02M to 9.44M. Power/scheduling was uncontrolled; growth
conclusions use counts, and recommendations use these observed medians.
