D. The Shift Queue (Top-K)
Three custom ways produce the same ordered queue: hardened full sort plus the K-prefix, a size-K heap with its worst retained case at the root, and a bounded
binary-search insertion buffer. The heap is a min-heap of priority (band, Amount, -Time, -txn_id), equivalently a max-heap under the best-first comparator. The
full sort is now measured at every K, including its actual prefix copy. Heap/buffer comparison loops are unchanged; their earlier five-run measurements remain
identified by provenance. The grid is K={10,50,52,500,2068,5000,n/10}, both required sizes and C1/C2. Class is evaluated only after ranking.

E. The Live Feed
Consecutive Time windows start at the first transaction. After each window, each strategy publishes the top K of all records seen: A re-ranks the prefix, B updates
its maintained heap, C sorts the new window and merges its ranked candidates. Every publication is verified after its timer stops. All strategies were remeasured
in this revision. Simulation totals and curves now use the same sum of algorithm-only window times; five individual totals are retained. Memory is measured
separately.
